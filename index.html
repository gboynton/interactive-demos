Interactive Teaching Demos

Browser-based demonstrations for statistics, perception, geometry, and related teaching. The source code is publicly available at GitHub: gboynton.

Statistics Vision & Perception Geometry Physics & Games

These demonstrations are intended for classroom use and exploration. Most are self-contained HTML/JavaScript pages: choose a module below to launch it, or follow the source link to inspect, download, or season to taste.
